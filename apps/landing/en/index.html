What it does for you

Six reasons to move your store to Norexos.

A register built for the counter

Search by name or code, take cash, card, bank transfer or credit, and hand over a printed receipt, WhatsApp or email.

A blind cash count you can trust

The system never fills in an amount for you: you count what really came in and went out, with a reason when there is a difference.

Inventory that does not lie

Every sale reduces stock; alerts for low stock and expiry dates before something runs out or spoils.

Credit and cash on delivery under control

Record what a customer owes and collect payments when they arrive, with manager approval when needed.

Sales reports

See what sells, what moves and how your register is doing, without spreadsheets.

With or without internet

The register keeps selling offline and syncs when the connection returns; your business does not stop.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No; it runs in the browser. To sell without internet you can install it on your PC.

Does it work for a single store?

Yes. It handles a store and a warehouse (transfers between them).

Can I sell on credit?

Yes, with manager approval above the limit.

What if the power or internet goes out?

The register keeps selling offline and syncs when it is back.

Is support available?

Support is currently provided in Spanish. Write to soporte@norexos.net.
